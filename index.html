About the academy

More than learning how the pieces move.

Shem Messo Chess Academy turns chess into a practical classroom for focus, courage and better decisions.

Each learner begins with strong foundations: how the board works, why a move matters and how to form a plan. Lessons then progress into tactics, opening ideas, endgames and the composure needed for competitive play.

Programs can run within partner schools, through focused private sessions or as preparation for inter-school tournaments.

The learning path

A clear journey from curiosity to confidence.

Players progress at a sensible pace, with every stage reinforcing the habits needed for the next.

Understand

Learn the board, the pieces and the principles that make a move useful.

Recognise

Spot tactical patterns, threats and opportunities before they disappear.

Plan

Connect individual moves into a clear idea and adapt when the position changes.

Compete

Play with discipline, manage time and learn constructively from every game.

Tournament support

Prepared players make calmer decisions.

Competition becomes less intimidating when a learner understands the format, knows how to use the clock and has practised responding to pressure.
