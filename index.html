Why Kericho Chess

A club built around thinking, not just playing

Every session is planned, every learner is tracked, and every tournament is a chance to measure progress.

Coached, not supervised

Structured lessons with a plan for each term — openings, tactics, endgames and clock handling, in that order.

In your school

We bring boards, clocks and worksheets to partner schools across Kericho, Litein and the wider county.

Measured on the circuit

Internal ladders feed into county and national events, so progress is recorded rather than assumed.

The Coaches

Coached by the people who run the club

Every session is taken by the academy's own office bearers — the same people who organise the county circuit and select the squads. No visiting coaches, no rotating volunteers.

  • One head coach across every program, so the teaching sequence holds together
  • Squad members have represented Kenya internationally
  • A named coach for every partner school
