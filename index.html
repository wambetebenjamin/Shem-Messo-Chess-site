OUR STORY

A little chess.
A lifetime of possibility.

At Kericho Chess Club & Academy, we believe a chessboard can open up a whole new world. We bring people together to learn, play, and grow—both on and off the board.

From curious beginners to aspiring competitors, our community is a place to develop patience, practise creative thinking, and discover what you’re capable of.

“Forward ever, backward never.”OUR MOTTO. OUR MINDSET.
