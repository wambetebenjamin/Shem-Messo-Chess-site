Kericho on the world stage

Season 26 · Academy news

Our Secretary travelled with the national squad to the Olympiad in Samarkand — the furthest a Kericho Chess Club badge has travelled, and a useful reminder to every learner in the county of where the game can take them.

The squad's preparation was ordinary: club ladders on Saturdays, endgame drills on Sundays, and a lot of games played honestly against better opposition.
