Registration.

Complete the official membership form, then review photo and video consent where applicable. Parents and guardians should complete one consent form for each child.

Before you begin

  1. Have your membership details ready. The membership form describes email verification; follow the instructions there.
  2. The supplied form says fees are shown at checkout, membership is inactive until paid, and a membership ID is issued after processing. We don’t collect payments or issue membership IDs on this website.
  3. The form currently states paid membership runs until 31 December 2026. Confirm current terms and fees with the academy.
  4. Photo/video permission is a separate choice, not the same as membership or a website play account.

Membership registration

Follow the official form’s instructions for details, verification and payment. A play account does not register you as an academy member.

This form is provided and processed by Google. Load it below to send your details directly to the academy’s supplied Google Form. This website cannot see or verify your submission.

Photo & video consent

The supplied form covers internal coaching/records and publicity uses. Permission to identify a child by name is separate. You may request a review or change/withdraw consent in writing; previously printed material and third-party shares cannot always be recalled.

Academy updates, if you want them.

Opt into browser push notifications for academy announcements and confirmed events. This is optional and separate from membership registration. We won’t ask permission until you choose to enable updates.

Checking notification support…

HTTPS and a supported browser are required. On iPhone/iPad, you may need to add this site to your home screen first. Announcements are sent by the academy; submitting a Google Form does not automatically trigger a push notification.
