How Entry Works

Three steps from reading the fixture to sitting at the board. Nothing is posted anywhere without your say-so.

01

Fill the entry form

Open the event page and complete the registration form. Two minutes, no account needed.

02

Pay the entry fee

M-Pesa Paybill 880100, account 123003#YourName. Keep the confirmation message.

03

Get confirmed

Shem confirms your section on WhatsApp and shares the venue card before the first round.

Every player under 18 needs a signed consent form. We photograph and film our events for coaching review, the club website and tournament reports. One consent form covers a player for the whole season.
