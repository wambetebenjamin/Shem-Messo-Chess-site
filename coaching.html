The Method: How A Learner Becomes A Player

A four-phase progression refined across dozens of school terms. Every learner knows exactly which phase they are in, and what the next one demands.

Phase 01 · Weeks 1 to 3

How the pieces think

Not just how they move, but what each piece wants. Value, activity, king safety and the board's geography, taught through mini-games instead of lectures.

Phase 02 · Weeks 4 to 8

Tactics every single day

Forks, pins, skewers and discovered attacks, drilled in short bursts. Learners solve puzzles on the site's Play page and on Lichess between sessions.

Phase 03 · Weeks 9 to 12

Openings with a plan

Principles over memorisation: centre, development, castle early. Each learner leaves the phase with one opening they truly understand with both colours.

Phase 04 · Season

Composure under the clock

Full tournament simulations with clocks and scoresheets, then the real circuit. Post-event analysis turns every loss into next season's points.
