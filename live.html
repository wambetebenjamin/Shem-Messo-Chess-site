BEHIND THE FEED

One laptop,
one board, one hall

The broadcast setup is deliberately simple: a board on a trestle table, a laptop relaying the moves, and Shem calling the position for anyone watching from home.

Boards 1 to 4 go out live on fixture days. Between fixtures the room replays academy study games, so there is nearly always something on.

  • Live clocks and evaluation bar, updated move by move
  • Spectator feed for parents, relatives and whole classes
  • Replays of study games on non-fixture evenings
