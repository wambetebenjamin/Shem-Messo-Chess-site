How it runs

What a program actually looks like

The school club, week by week

One hour, one theme, one habit. We open with a short taught point on the demonstration board, then learners play it out on their own boards while the coach walks the room.

  • 10 minutes of teaching on one idea
  • 30 minutes of coached games
  • 15 minutes reviewing one game on the demo board
  • 5 minutes recording results for the term report

The teaching sequence

Every learner moves through the same order, at their own pace. Nobody gets tactics before they can see a hanging piece, and nobody gets openings before they understand development.

  1. Board craft — how the pieces move, and how to see the whole board.
  2. Basic tactics — forks, pins, skewers, and the hanging piece.
  3. Opening principles — development, the centre, and king safety.
  4. Endgames — king and pawn, then the basic mates.
  5. Clock and competition — playing a rated game properly, start to finish.
