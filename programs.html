JUNIOR CHESS

Junior chess academy

Learn how the pieces move, understand the rules, and practise basic tactics with a coach.

CLUB CHESS

Club & competitive chess

Play other club members, review your games, and work on your tournament preparation.

SCHOOL CHESS

Chess in schools

Interested in chess at your school? Talk to us about introducing pupils to the game.

HOLIDAY SEASON

Chess, games and dancing.

Fun activities for juniors, families and friends during the holidays.

CHESS SCRAMBLE

Chess scramble

A fast, friendly chess scramble: quick rounds that keep everyone moving and sharpen tactical awareness. Great for beginners and experienced players alike.

INDOOR GAMES

Other indoor games

Board and table games beyond chess for the holiday break. Family-friendly activities that keep young players busy, learning and laughing together indoors.

DANCING

Dancing

Joyful indoor dancing sessions to move, have fun and celebrate the season with the academy community. Everyone is welcome to join in at their own pace.
