Welcome To Kericho Chess Club & Academy

Kericho Chess Club & Academy brings together people who want to learn and play chess. We welcome beginners and players looking to practise and prepare for competition.

Our programs cover junior coaching, club chess and chess in schools. Learning the rules, practising tactics and reviewing games are all part of a thoughtful chess journey.

“Forward ever, backward never.” Join our community and find your next move.
