Home Consent

Dear Parents and Guardians,

During training sessions, tournaments, school visits and club events, we take photos and videos of our players. We would like your permission to use these images in the ways described below.

Internal use: training and coaching review, club records, notice boards, and newsletters shared with members and parents.

Publicity and communication: the club website, social media pages, brochures, posters, tournament reports, and sponsor or partner communications.

Your child's privacy

  • Children are not identified by full name in publicity materials. First names or full names may be used only if you give separate permission below — for example when announcing tournament results or awards.
  • You may ask to review any photo or video of your child used for publicity.
  • You may withdraw or change your consent at any time by notifying us in writing. We will stop using the images going forward and remove them from our platforms where we can. We cannot recall materials already printed or shared by third parties.
  • Images are stored securely and used only for the purposes above.

Please complete one form for each child.
