About the championship

Kericho Chess Club & Academy invites players from across the county to the Kericho County Open Chess Championship 2026, a one-day tournament built for competitive players and for those playing their very first rated event.

The championship brings together schools, clubs and independent players for a full day of competition. Every entrant plays every round — there are no knock-outs and nobody is eliminated — so a player who loses the first game is still in contention for the prizes.

7 rounds · Swiss pairing Paired by rating, re-paired after every round so you meet players on your score.
30 + 0 time control Thirty minutes each on digital clocks. Clocks, boards and scoresheets are provided.
Licensed arbiters The event is run by trained arbiters under standard Swiss rules.
Top boards streamed The leading boards are broadcast live so parents can follow from home.

Awards & Recognition

Trophies will be awarded to the 1st, 2nd and 3rd positions in every section:

  • Open — trophies plus the county champion's title
  • Ladies — trophies and county ranking points
  • Juniors (U-18) — trophies and selection consideration for the county squad
  • Cadets (U-12) — trophies and medals
  • School teams — a shield for the best-placed school

In addition, certificates will be awarded to all participants, teachers, schools and coaches, and every entrant earns circuit points towards the end-of-season honour roll.

More Than Chess

Tournament chess teaches things a classroom rarely can: how to sit with a hard position, how to plan under a running clock, and how to shake hands and reset after a loss. Our events are deliberately run as full-day experiences — an opening talk, seven rounds, and a closing ceremony where every single player is named.

Players leave with a scoresheet they can review with a coach, a certificate, and a clear picture of what to work on next. Every move counts — on the board and off it.
