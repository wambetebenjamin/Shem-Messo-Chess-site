Why join

Membership is what makes the club run

Subscriptions pay for the boards in partner schools, the clocks on tournament day and the kit that travels to every fixture. In return, members get the CKF rate at our events, first refusal on places, and a record of every game they play.

  • CKF member rate at every academy tournament
  • First refusal on places when an event fills up
  • A rating tracked across the internal ladder
  • Access to the club library of boards and books
  • Discounted private lessons and coaching materials
