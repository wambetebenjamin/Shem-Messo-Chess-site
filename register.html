How To Become a Member

Three steps and you are on the register. Members get county voting rights, access to national events and subsidised entry to every club-sanctioned tournament.

01

Fill out the form

Your personal details, your category and how you want us to reach you.

02

Verify your email

Email is our primary means of communication, so we ask you to confirm the address.

03

Pay the membership fee

Presented at checkout. Pay now and membership runs to 31.12.2026; pay later and it stays inactive until payment.

Membership benefits: participation in County Chess Association AGMs, access to national events, and subsidies on all club-sanctioned events.
