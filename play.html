Notation

Coach's note: before every move, name your opponent's threat out loud. Ten minutes here beats an hour of casual play.

Castling, en passant and promotion are all handled by the board, and so is the other side of it: the engine in js/engine.js searches this position in the background. Hint shows you the move it would have played. The same drills our learners run between coaching sessions.
