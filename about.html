About Us

One board, carried between schools

Kericho Chess Club & Academy is a county club and coaching school: school programmes, private lessons, tournament preparation and the kit to sustain all three.

Our Mission

Forward Ever, Backward Never

Three pieces stand on our crest: the pawn that begins the journey, the king that must be protected, and the knight that finds the way around. Under them sits the rule we coach by — every learner moves forward, one square at a time.

We exist to give Kericho learners real coaching instead of a borrowed lunch hour. That means a planned lesson, a board each, and a record of every game played.

  • Deep thinking. Every session teaches a habit of looking one move further.
  • Composure. Losing a queen is not losing a game. We coach the recovery, not just the opening.
  • Sportsmanship. The handshake matters as much as the result.
Our Story

From one board to a county circuit

The question was always the same: what happens when children get real coaching?

  1. Season 24

    A single board, carried between schools

    The academy began with one set and a bus timetable, running lunch-hour sessions wherever a head teacher would lend a room.

  2. Season 25

    Clubs that sfield full squads

    Schools that started with four curious learners began entering teams at inter-school fixtures. The weekly Saturday ladder followed.

  3. Season 26

    On the national and international stage

    Academy members took their games to the county circuit and beyond — our Secretary represented Kenya in Samarkand — and parents began following live from their phones.

  4. Today

    Coaching, competition and the kit to sustain both

    12 partner schools, 340 learners trained and 15 tournaments run. The academy now covers the full journey a school club takes, from first lesson to first rated event.

Partner Schools

Twelve schools, one standard of coaching

We partner with primary and secondary schools in and around Kericho town, Litein and the wider county. Every partner gets the same thing: a taught weekly session, boards and clocks for the room, and a path into the county circuit.

  • Weekly taught session during the school day
  • Boards, clocks and worksheets provided
  • Entry into inter-school and county fixtures
  • Termly report on attendance and results
