About Kericho Chess Academy

One coach.
Sixty-four squares.

Shem Messo has spent years turning quiet Kericho classrooms into competitive chess clubs, one board, one learner, one school at a time.

Our Origin & Mission

Chess deserves more
than a spare period

The academy began with a single board carried between schools. The question was always the same: what happens when children get real coaching instead of a borrowed lunch hour?

The answer is on the results tables. Clubs that started with four curious learners now field full squads at inter-school fixtures. Learners who once watched from the corridor now stream their games to their parents' phones.

  • School coaching programmes
  • Tournament organizing & circuit fixtures
  • Professional materials & club supplies
Mentorship in Action Move by move growth
Core Academy Values

More than a game

Deep thinking

Every position is a small problem with real consequences. Learners carry the habit of "think first" straight back into classwork.

Composure

Clock ticking, crowd watching, a piece hanging, and still, breathe and find the move. That skill transfers to every exam room.

Sportsmanship

Shake hands, record the game, learn from the loss. The circuit runs on respect for opponents, arbiters and the game itself.
