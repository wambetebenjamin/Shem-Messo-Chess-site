ABOUT THE ACADEMY

A chess club
for our community.

Kericho Chess Club & Academy brings together people who want to learn and play chess. We welcome beginners and players looking to practise and prepare for competition.

Our programs cover junior coaching, club chess, and chess in schools. Contact the team to find out which sessions would suit you or your child.
