Learners sitting together at a competition

MOVE 4 · D4

A hall of quiet,
then a roar

Doors open at eight, boards are set by half past, and the room goes silent the moment the first clock starts. What follows is a full day of Swiss rounds, scoresheets and handshakes.

Parents who cannot travel watch the top four boards right here on the Live page, and every learner leaves with a result recorded against their name.

  • Every learner plays every round, nobody is knocked out
  • Clocks, boards and scoresheets supplied at the venue
  • Medals per category, trophies for the top three overall
