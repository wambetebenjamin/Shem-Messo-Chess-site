Competitor Experience

What Every Entrant Receives

We provide a world-class, professional tournament experience for young chess enthusiasts in Kericho County.

Tournament Sets & Boards

Full FIDE-approved weighted pieces and non-glare roll-up vinyl boards provided at every single board.

Digital Chess Clocks

Official DGT digital clocks configured with 15-minute time controls plus 10-second increments for fair matchplay.

Official Scoresheets & Pens

Personal scoresheets to record moves and analyze games with academy coaches between rounds.

Trophies & Medals

Gold, silver, and bronze trophies for the top 3 in every division, plus merit medals for top finishers.

Certificate of Participation

Official stamped Kericho Chess Academy certificates awarded to every participant recognising their sportsmanship.

Live Board Streaming

Top boards 1 through 4 relayed live on our broadcast room so family, teachers, and schoolmates can watch.
